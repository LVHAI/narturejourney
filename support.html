Frequently Asked Questions

How do I identify a plant?

From the Home screen, tap "Identify a Plant." Take a photo or choose one from your gallery, select the plant organ (or leave it on Auto), and tap Identify. Results appear in a few seconds.

How do I add a voice note?

After identifying a plant, tap "Add a Voice Note" on the result screen. You can record up to 5 minutes. Voice notes are completely optional — you can save any observation without one.

Where is my data stored?

All your photos, voice notes, and observations are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to any server. Plant photos are sent to Pl@ntNet for identification and are not stored by us.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Settings, then tap "Restore Purchases." Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID, so it will restore automatically on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your device Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → NatureJourney, then tap "Cancel Subscription."

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete All Data. This permanently removes all observations, photos, and voice notes from your device. Deleting the app also removes all data.

Why do I have a daily identification limit?

Free users can identify up to 2 plants per day. NatureJourney Pro subscribers can identify up to 100 plants per day. This limit helps us keep the service sustainable.

The app can't identify my plant. What should I do?

Try taking a clearer photo with good lighting, and make sure the plant organ (leaf, flower, fruit) is well-centered. If you know what part you photographed, select it manually instead of using Auto.
