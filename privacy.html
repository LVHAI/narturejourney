Privacy Policy

Your privacy is not a feature. It's the foundation.

Summary: NatureJourney stores all your photos, voice notes, and observations only on your device. We do not have servers that store your data. The only exception is that plant photos are sent to Pl@ntNet for identification.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

NatureJourney does not collect, store, or transmit the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Your device identifier (IDFA, IDFV)
  • Your contacts, calendar, or health data
  • Any analytics or crash reports with personal information
  • Advertising identifiers or behavioral data

2. Information Stored on Your Device

All of the following are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server:

  • Plant photos you capture or select
  • Voice notes you record (up to 5 minutes each)
  • Species names, dates, and any notes you add
  • Your daily identification usage count
  • Your theme preference (light / dark / system)

This data is encrypted at rest by iOS Data Protection and is removed when you delete the app or use Settings → Delete All Data.

3. Third-Party Services

NatureJourney uses the following third-party service:

Pl@ntNet — When you identify a plant, the photo you selected is sent to Pl@ntNet's identification API. Pl@ntNet processes this image to return species suggestions. We do not store the image on our servers. Pl@ntNet's own privacy practices are governed by their policy at plantnet.org.

Apple In-App Purchase — Subscription payments are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics, or ad networks are used in this app.

4. Camera and Microphone

NatureJourney requests the following permissions:

  • Camera — to photograph plants for identification
  • Photo Library — to select an existing photo for identification
  • Microphone — to record optional voice notes, stored only on your device

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. The app will continue to work with reduced functionality.

5. Children's Privacy

NatureJourney does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because NatureJourney does not collect personal data on any server, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete upon request. All data is under your direct control on your device. You can:

  • Delete individual observations from the History tab
  • Delete all data via Settings → Delete All Data
  • Remove all data by uninstalling the app

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
