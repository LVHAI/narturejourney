About NatureJourney

Identify plants. Record moments. Stay private.

NatureJourney is a private nature journal that combines plant identification with voice notes. Whether you're a casual walker, a gardener, or a naturalist, NatureJourney helps you remember not just what you saw, but how it felt.

Why NatureJourney?

  • Identify plants instantly — powered by Pl@ntNet, one of the world's leading plant identification services.
  • Add voice notes — capture up to 5 minutes of your thoughts, the weather, the smell, the moment.
  • 100% local storage — your photos, voice notes, and observations never leave your device.
  • No account required — open the app and start recording. No sign-up, no email, no tracking.
  • No location tracking — we don't ask for location permissions. Your walks stay private.
  • Beautiful, distraction-free design — built for the outdoors, not the feed.

How It Works

1. Identify. Take a photo of any plant. NatureJourney sends it to Pl@ntNet and returns the most likely species within seconds.

2. Record. Optionally add a voice note — up to 5 minutes — describing what you see, hear, or feel. Or skip it. It's up to you.

3. Remember. Every observation is saved to your private journal. Browse them in a beautiful masonry grid, play back your voice notes, and revisit your moments in nature.

Pricing

NatureJourney is free to use with up to 2 identifications per day. NatureJourney Pro unlocks up to 100 identifications per day for $3.99/month, cancel anytime.

Our Privacy Promise

We built NatureJourney with a "privacy by design" philosophy. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ad networks, and no tracking. The only data that ever leaves your device is the plant photo you submit for identification — and it is never stored by us.
